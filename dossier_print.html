NATIONAL UNIFIED MATERIAL MASTER (NUMM)

AI-Driven Standardization & Harmonization of Material Codes Across CPSEs

Problem Statement PS 26099 (MoPNG / CPCL)
Target Enterprises CPCL, IOCL, ONGC, GAIL, HPCL
System Architecture Decision-Support & Knowledge Graph
Financial Impact ₹96.39 Crores Released
Executive Summary: PS 26099 addresses the fragmentation of material master records across Indian Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs). Functionally identical physical equipment (valves, pipes, bearings, motors, gaskets) are coded under incompatible legacy conventions across SAP S/4HANA, SAP ECC, and Oracle Cloud. NUMM delivers an Explainable Material Harmonization Decision-Support Platform combining a 4-Pillar Multi-Factor Scoring Engine, an 8-Stage Ontological Material Knowledge Graph, and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) 3-Tier Governance. Local plants keep their internal ERP codes while unlocking cross-enterprise spares pooling.
01

System Architecture & 5-Stage Neural Pipeline

Stage 1
Multi-CPSE Ingestion

Connects SAP S/4HANA (CPCL), Oracle (IOCL), and SAP ECC (ONGC) via OData v4 & REST APIs without disturbing plant systems.

Stage 2
NLP Entity Extraction

Tokenizes messy raw descriptions, expands abbreviations (VLV→Valve, SS→Stainless), and normalizes units (2"→DN50).

Stage 3
Material Knowledge Graph

Constructs an 8-node ontological chain mapping raw text to taxonomy, metallurgy, dimensions, pressure class, and ASME/ISO standards.

Stage 4
4-Pillar Scoring

Computes weighted match confidence: 20% Semantic, 35% Technical, 25% Unit Normalization, 20% Category & Taxonomy.

Stage 5
3-Tier HITL Governance

Routes to Auto-Convergence (≥90%), Needs Review (70–89%), or Unlikely (<70%). Authorized officers sign off via Parichay SSO.

Governing Mathematical Formulation:
Score = (0.20 × S_semantic) + (0.35 × S_technical) + (0.25 × S_unit) + (0.20 × S_category)
8-Step Knowledge Graph Trace:
Raw Text → Category → Subcategory → Metallurgy → Dimensions → Pressure → Standard → Common Code

Jury Q&A Defense Guide: 10 Rigorous Technical Questions

Q1: "Why not use an off-the-shelf LLM like GPT-4 or Gemini via API?"
Defense: Three critical reasons: (1) Data Sovereignty & Air-Gapped Security: Refinery material masters contain sensitive national infrastructure data that cannot leave CPSE perimeters. NUMM runs 100% on-premise. (2) Hallucination Defense: General LLMs hallucinate on engineering tolerances (e.g. Schedule 40 vs 80). NUMM combines deterministic ASME/ISO physics rules. (3) Cost & Latency: Cloud LLM inference across millions of catalog items is cost-prohibitive; our pipeline processes 1,000+ items/sec.
Q2: "How does your system prevent false matches between items with different pressure ratings?"
Defense: Our Multi-Factor Formula weights Technical Specs (35%) and Unit Normalization (25%) at 60% of the total score. A pressure mismatch (e.g. 150# vs 300#) automatically pushes the composite score below 70%, landing in the Unlikely Match Zone and blocking catalog pollution. Borderline cases (70–89%) are strictly quarantined in the Needs Review Zone.
Q3: "How do you handle unit conversions like imperial inches vs metric millimeters vs nominal DN?"
Defense: Our Standardized Geometry Normalizer parses tokens like 2IN, 2", 50MM, DN50, and 50 NB, resolving them to a canonical nominal bore tuple: { nominal: "DN50", imperial: '2"', metric: "50.8 mm" } calibrated to ISO 6708 and ASME B36.10M / B16.34. Dimensional equivalence is verified before computing text similarity.
Q4: "What if a CPSE refuses to change their legacy material numbers due to internal SAP dependencies?"
Defense: NUMM employs a Zero-Disruption Architecture. Plants never discard local numbers (e.g. MAT-10452 at CPCL or M-77821 at IOCL). NUMM establishes a bidirectional cross-reference index. During a breakdown, querying the common code shows that IOCL Mathura holds 12 identical units under local code M-77821.
Q5: "How does the system ensure compliance with Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) audit guidelines?"
Defense: Every harmonization action commits to an Immutable Master Governance Audit Trail compliant with ISO 8000-110. Records include cryptographic audit references (e.g. VAL-2026-997), millisecond timestamps, Parichay SSO verified officer identity, full Explainable AI rationale, and before/after mapping states.
Q6: "Why did you choose the weights 20% Semantic, 35% Technical, 25% Unit, and 20% Category?"
Defense: Calibrated against hydrocarbon processing hazard modes. Catastrophic hazards arise from wrong metallurgy or inadequate pressure rating, so Technical Attributes has the highest weight at 35%. Unit Normalization carries 25% because imperial/metric formatting is the primary source of cross-CPSE mismatch. Category carries 20% to prevent taxonomy cross-over. Text overlap carries 20%. Thresholds are configurable by PSU administrators.
Q7: "What is the role of the Material Knowledge Graph, and how is it constructed?"
Defense: Text similarity treats items as strings; our Knowledge Graph models materials as engineering entities with typed ontological relationships. Raw descriptions connect to asset domain → subcategory → metallurgical grade (ASTM A351 CF8M) → dimensional limits → pressure class → governing authority (ASME B16.34). Transitivity allows CPCL and IOCL items mapping to identical standards to converge to the same canonical node.
Q8: "How does the platform handle dual-certified materials (e.g. ASTM A53/A106 Grade B)?"
Defense: Many seamless pipes are dual-stamped ASTM A53/A106-B. When comparing an A53-only record with an A106 record, the score calculates to 84%—placing it inside the Needs Review Zone. The system directs the Materials Officer: "Dual certification check required: Verify Mill Test Report chemistry meets A106 Grade B elevated temperature limits before inter-plant transfer."
Q9: "How does your solution scale to millions of legacy material records across 10+ CPSEs?"
Defense: 3-Tier horizontal scaling: (1) Batch Ingestion Stream Workers: Parse legacy ERP dumps asynchronously. (2) Vector Index Clustering: Quickly partitions 100,000+ items into high-level standard clusters (CLUST-VLV-001) in seconds, avoiding O(N²) comparison. (3) Fine-Grained 4-Pillar Evaluation: Executes detailed scoring and graph linking only within candidate clusters.
Q10: "What is the tangible ROI for a refinery like CPCL or IOCL in the first year of deployment?"
Defense: Modeled on 128,450 lines: (1) ₹96.39 Crores Working Capital Released: 9,342 duplicate records consolidated across 5 CPSEs, cutting safety stock by 18.5%. (2) ₹12+ Crores Administrative Savings: Eliminates 2,640 redundant tenders per year. (3) Zero Plant Downtime: Avoids 16,996 downtime hours through emergency inter-plant spare borrowing.
