THE PROBLEM

Fragmented CPSE Material Data

Cross-CPSE Industrial Challenge

CPSEs across Oil & Gas, Power, Steel, Mining & Heavy Engineering maintain vast, isolated material master databases with inconsistent item codes, varying naming conventions, disparate technical specifications, non-standard units of measurement (UOM), and heterogeneous ERP/SAP systems.

“Functionally identical physical components appear as completely unrelated records across CPSE inventories, paralyzing cross-enterprise discovery and bulk procurement synergies.”

Example: The Same Material Recorded Differently Across CPSEs Illustrative Example

Resulting Consequences • The Enterprise Cost of Disconnection
