Zero Silent Commits

Unlike black-box AI tools, NUMM enforces a strict Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) governance gate. AI algorithms generate confidence scores and explainable engineering breakdowns, but only authorized CPSE Materials Officers can approve catalog convergence.

🛡️

3-Tier Routing Protocol

Candidate matches are routed through 3 configurable zones: Auto-Convergence (≥90%) for 1-click approval, Needs Review (70%–89%) for dual-certified cases (e.g. ASTM A53 vs A106 temp envelope), and Unlikely Match (<70%) to block catalog pollution.

🔄

Zero ERP Disruption

CPCL, IOCL, and ONGC engineers do not discard their existing SAP or Oracle material numbers. NUMM builds a universal bidirectional cross-reference index so plants can discover and borrow spares across enterprises seamlessly.
